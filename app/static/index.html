Routing accuracy

how well the AI classifier itself is routing tickets, not resolution quality

Correction rate is the share of tickets a human had to re-route to a different category — the most direct signal of classifier accuracy. Zoho agreement compares our prediction against whatever category the ticket already carried in Zoho, where one exists. Corrections come from two sources: someone clicking "Correct" in this portal, or someone transferring the ticket to a different category/team directly in Zoho — both teach the classifier the same way, see the "source" breakdown below.
